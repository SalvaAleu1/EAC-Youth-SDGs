About the platform

From regional learning to practical community action.

The EAC Youth SDGs Platform is a youth-led regional initiative promoting SDG awareness, understanding and meaningful participation across East Africa. We make the Sustainable Development Goals practical through digital engagement, community outreach, storytelling, learning spaces and dialogue.

Our work responds to two connected realities: many communities still lack meaningful digital access, while knowledge of the SDGs and their relevance to everyday life remains limited. We therefore combine digital inclusion with climate and SDG education so access to information leads to participation and action.

Our focus

Four connected areas of action.

We are keeping the platform focused on the original regional action plan: awareness, digital equality, climate education and participation.

◎

SDG Awareness

Helping young people and communities understand the SDGs, their rights-based foundations and how the Goals connect to everyday life.

⌘

Digital Equality

Strengthening digital literacy and meaningful use of technology, especially among rural, marginalized and underserved communities.

◒

Climate Action

Making climate information accessible and practical, with attention to adaptation, environmental responsibility and local solutions.

↗

Youth Participation

Creating spaces for youth voice, civic engagement, community dialogue, regional collaboration and participation in SDG implementation.
